# Client Design Requirements — Sheet Metal Bracket Family

**Client:** Sample Industries Inc.
**Document ID:** SPEC-BRK-001
**Revision:** A

## 1. Material Requirements
All bracket parts shall be manufactured from one of the following approved materials:
Aluminum 6061, Aluminum 5052, or Stainless Steel 304. Use of any other material
requires written client approval.

## 2. Wall Thickness
Minimum wall thickness for aluminum brackets shall be 2.5 mm to ensure adequate
structural rigidity under expected load conditions.

## 3. Mounting Hole Tolerance
All mounting hole positions shall be held to a tolerance of ±0.1 mm from nominal
position to ensure compatibility with client's mating hardware.

## 4. Envelope / Size Limit
The overall bounding box of any bracket part shall not exceed 200 mm x 150 mm x 100 mm,
per the client's enclosure constraints.

## 5. Edge and Fillet Requirements
All internal edges and fillets shall have a minimum radius of 1.0 mm to reduce stress
concentration and avoid sharp-edge safety hazards during handling.
